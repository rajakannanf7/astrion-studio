What you can book

Just the space,
or the whole crew.

Some people want an empty room and four hours of quiet. Some want to walk in, sit down, and walk out with a month of content. Both are fine.

Studio Rental

Book the cyc wall or either podcast room by the hour, half day or full day. Strobes, modifiers, stands and mics are included — you bring the camera.

  • Hourly, half-day and full-day slots
  • Strobes, modifiers and C-stands included
  • Paper and canvas backdrops
  • Parking and ground-floor loading

Rates on request — ask for the rate card

Personal Branding

A half day on the cyc wall that gives you a quarter of content. Headshots, talking-head video, vertical reels and B-roll — shot, cut and handed over.

  • Headshots & portrait sets
  • Talking head with teleprompter
  • Vertical reels for social
  • Hair, makeup and styling on request

Rates on request — half and full day

Podcast Production

Two rooms, up to four mics, one operator. Walk in with your guests, walk out with a recorded episode and the clips already pulled.

  • Two seats in Room One, four in Room Two
  • Broadcast mics, isolated tracks
  • Audio clean-up and mix
  • Short-form clips cut from the episode

Rates on request — per hour or per episode

How it works

Book it in
four steps.

No account, no app. Send the date and the room, and we reply the same day.

  1. 01

    Pick a room

    Tell us the room, the date and how many hours. We hold the slot for 24 hours while you decide.

  2. 02

    Lock the slot

    Half up front confirms the booking. Reschedule free up to 48 hours before your call time.

  3. 03

    Shoot day

    Doors open 30 minutes before your slot for setup, free of charge. A studio assistant is on site all day.

  4. 04

    Files in 48h

    Booked production too? Selects land the next morning, the full edit within two working days.

The studio

A proper studio,
close to home.

Astrion exists because getting to a real cyc wall used to mean a day of travel and a night away. So we built one here in Surandai — 1,600 square feet of seamless wall, high enough to fly a light properly.

Next to it are two treated podcast rooms: one for a host and a guest, one for a four-person table. Everything is on one floor, everything is on wheels, and someone is always on site.
